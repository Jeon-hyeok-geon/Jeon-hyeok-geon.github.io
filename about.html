I am a software engineering student interested in the boundary between AI workloads, data analysis, and storage systems.

Current direction

My current personal research explores the system-side workload created when coding agents inspect repositories, modify code, and run tests. I am especially interested in how those tool-execution paths appear across process, filesystem, and storage-device measurements.

How I present work

I separate a question from a conclusion, ongoing work from finished work, and a team outcome from my own contribution. This portfolio is intended to show the evidence and limits behind each project, not to make a list of unverified claims.

Areas of interest

  • AI systems and agent workloads
  • Data analysis and predictive maintenance
  • Storage I/O, system measurement, and reliability
